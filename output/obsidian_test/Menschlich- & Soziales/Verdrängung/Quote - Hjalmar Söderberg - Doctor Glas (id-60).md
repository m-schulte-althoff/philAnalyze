# Quote: Hjalmar Söderberg - Doctor Glas

> This last astounded me. I just sat there, without a word to reply. Right up to the moment I heard my own name uttered I thought he was speaking of himself. And I still think so. It was simply in order to conceal it that he pounced upon me. An oppressive silence followed. I looked out over the glittering waters of The Stream.
