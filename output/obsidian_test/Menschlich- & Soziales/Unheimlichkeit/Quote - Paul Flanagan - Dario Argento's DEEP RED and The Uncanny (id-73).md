# Quote: Paul Flanagan - Dario Argento's DEEP RED and The Uncanny

> Freud's ideas on the uncanny: "That which is undoubtedly related to what is frightening - to what arouses dread and horror." (Sigmund Freud, “The Uncanny”). Barbara Creed arranges Freud's ideas into three categories: (i) Those things that relate to the notion of a double or doppelganger, a multiplied object; a ghost or a spirit; an involuntary act. (Related to a discrete entity) (ii) Castration anxieties expressed as fear of the female genitals or of dismembered limbs, a severed head or hand, loss of the eyes. (Related to gender) (iii) A feeling associated with a familiar/unfamiliar place, losing one's way, womb phantasies, a haunted house. (Related to the known vs. the unknowable)
